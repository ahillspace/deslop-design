Operations pilot: decision material

Decision requested

The committee is asked to authorize two additional weeks of the intake-and-dispatch pilot and up to $1,200 for temporary evening coverage. The request is not yet approved. The team proposes reviewing the extended pilot before making a permanent staffing decision.

What the log contains

The review covers three weekday shifts over nine calendar days in October 2026. The intake log contains 46 requests. Fifteen of the 46 requests have a recorded wait longer than 30 minutes. The reported median wait is 23 minutes among 43 requests with a dispatch timestamp. Three requests have no dispatch timestamp and are excluded from that median.

What the comparison can support

The log describes the pilot period. It does not compare a randomized control group or establish that the pilot caused faster dispatch. The recorded waits may not cover every request received during the period. This is an operational sample, not a full service-level measurement.

A useful next step

If the committee extends the pilot, record all received requests, dispatch timestamps, shift coverage, and exclusions for the full two weeks. Compare the same wait definition across the pilot period and the preceding four weeks, and review missing timestamps before considering a permanent change. This is a proposed measurement plan, not a completed study.
